Overview
The DP ranks of a MoE engine step in lockstep. When any rank has prefill, the step becomes a long mixed (and, on some backends, eager) step for every rank. Agentic multi-turn traffic makes prefill frequent, so decode keeps getting interrupted.
--dp-prefill-delayer
(opt-in, default off) aligns prefill across the ranks and protects the decode steps after a prefill. It follows ATOM's PrefillDelayer.
Claims
Measured on a development branch with an earlier version of this change: DeepSeek-V4-Pro on 8x MI355X, DP8 attention + EP8, AgentX agentic workload, 1200 s runs, metrics over 300-1200 s, single runs per configuration.

Details
Protocol.
Once per engine step, each rank reports 6 ints in one CPU SUM all-reduce on the DP group: prefillable, pending prefill tokens (capped at
max_num_batched_tokens
), running decodes, KV usage >= 0.9, chunk in progress, prefill ran. Every rank then takes the same FIRE / HOLD decision for its next schedule.
Placement.
The all-reduce sits next to
_has_global_unfinished_reqs()
. Every rank reaches that point on every non-idle step (real, dummy and pause steps alike), which the existing DP state sync already relies on, so the extra collective cannot be skipped by one rank. The state resets at each DP wave boundary.
Rules (ATOM's), in order:
A step that ran prefill arms
--dp-prefill-delayer-protected-steps
(default 10) steps that HOLD while decodes run.
FIRE if no rank has prefill, no rank decodes, or KV usage is high.
FIRE after 200 held steps, or after 100 with a chunk in progress.
HOLD until all ranks have prefill.
FIRE when the pending tokens reach 0.9 x prefillable ranks x
max_num_batched_tokens
, or when the backlog stops growing for 10 steps.
HOLD scope.
HOLD defers all prefill compute, new requests and in-progress chunks, even on a rank without local decodes, so that ranks prefill together.
Interaction with
--prefill-schedule-interval
.
The delayer replaces it; setting both is an error.
Cost.
Off: none. On: one 6-int CPU all-reduce per step.
Not supported:
elastic EP and custom scheduler classes.

[Core][DP] Add an opt-in cross-DP prefill delayer
…
2766131
--dp-prefill-delayer (off by default) aligns prefill across the lockstep
ranks of a data-parallel MoE engine, following ATOM's PrefillDelayer: once
per engine step the ranks all-reduce a 6-int prefill state on the DP group
and all take the same FIRE / HOLD decision for the next step. A step that
ran prefill is followed by --dp-prefill-delayer-protected-steps (default 10)
decode-only steps; otherwise prefill waits until every rank has some and
the pending tokens fill 0.9 of the prefillable ranks' token budgets, with
bounds for no decode, KV usage, held steps (200, or 100 with a chunk in
progress) and a stalled backlog. The all-reduce runs where the existing DP
state sync runs, so every rank reaches it in the same steps. It replaces
--prefill-schedule-interval; setting both is an error.

Why
--prefill-schedule-interval
does not solve it here
The interval coordinates
when
the DP ranks may prefill: they share the step counter, so on off-cadence steps every rank defers prefill. It does not coordinate
whether
they prefill: each rank decides from its own queue and sees nothing of the other ranks. Under agentic load this still lets prefill reach most steps:
After a release step, a rank whose waiting queue did not fit the token budget (
prefill_capacity_bound
) stops throttling and prefills on every step until the queue drains. The check is local to the rank, but one prefilling rank makes the step mixed for all 8 ranks. With 90-130K-token prompts at c128, some rank is often in this state. This is our reading of the c128 result; we have not counted the mixed steps yet.
A cadence step runs whatever prefill each rank has, even one small chunk on one rank, and the other ranks pay a full mixed step for it.
The delayer adds the missing state exchange. Once per step the ranks all-reduce a few counters (pending prefill tokens, running decodes, KV pressure), and every rank takes the same decision. It fires when the pending tokens across the ranks reach about 90% of their budget, then keeps the next N steps decode-only.
Next steps
This PR is at an early stage. Our next goal is to lower TTFT, or at least bound it so that a TTFT SLA can be met:
A time-based bound: release when the oldest waiting prefill has waited longer than a configured budget in ms.
A sweep against interval 8 / 16 (with and without the long-prefill threshold) across concurrency, reported as throughput vs E2E-normalized interactivity and throughput vs TTFT.
A looser alignment is a first candidate. We tried releasing with 6-7 of 8 ranks ready and a shorter partial-chunk hold, on the current recipe and against the same-node default. At about the same throughput (-1.8% to +1.3%), TTFT went down 28-39% and the E2E-normalized p90 went up 29-42%, while the ITL-only p90 went down 15-24%.
Also related:
#54627
(just merged) has the same basic idea, holding prefill so that decode runs in longer stretches, but it applies only when data_parallel_size == 1. On MoE DP ranks it is a no-op. There, the existing cadence aligns when the ranks may prefill, but no rank sees the other ranks' queues. This PR adds that cross-rank state: one small all-reduce per step, so every rank takes the same prefill decision.
