Method and comparison boundary

  • Latency: native values come directly from merged summary.json; AIPerf values come from authoritative score.json. No latency is inferred.
  • Cost: 8 × GPU $/hour ÷ (completed req/s × 3,600 × utilization) × 1,000,000.
  • Same workload: frontiers are recomputed only inside the selected workload, cache boundary, load mode, and percentile. AIPerf trace speeds share one dataset view and are distinguished by marker shape.
  • Quality: non-comparable measurements remain available under “All measured points” but cannot enter a frontier.
