Why the offload
The Engram tables are 183 GiB of the checkpoint. Row-sharded in HBM on 141 GB cards they crowd out the KV pool at TP4. Moving them to pinned host DRAM, read through UVA with unchanged output, frees 23.6 GiB per rank, 94 GiB across the replica.
Measured on the InferenceX AgentX sweep, from the c1 server log of run
35494445931

--max-cudagraph-capture-size
