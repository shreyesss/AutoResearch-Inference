Motivation
The H200 [32,32] block-wise fp8_w8a8 tuned tables are missing several shapes and most M buckets. Decode and verify hit these shapes with small M; without a tuned entry the kernel falls back to an untuned config, and the M-key lookup snaps to the nearest bucket, so a gap of 6x between buckets shows up directly in step time.
Modifications
Adds 8 new H200 [32,32] fp8_w8a8 configs:
N=512,K=5120
N=1280,K=5120
N=1536,K=5120
N=32768,K=1280
N=5120,K=8192
N=5120,K=15360
N=4608,K=5120
N=5120,K=2304
Extends 7 existing H200 [32,32] fp8_w8a8 configs with additional M buckets (4,6,8,12,16,32,64,128,256,1024,2048), keeping the existing M=1 and M=2 entries unchanged:
N=1152,K=5120
N=1792,K=5120
N=25600,K=6144
N=4096,K=1280
N=5120,K=2048
N=5120,K=576
N=8192,K=1280
All files are additive; no existing config entry is modified. No M >= 512 entry carries SWAP_AB or SPLIT_K, so the prefill path is unaffected.
Accuracy Tests
Config-only change; no kernel or numerics change. The tuned configs select different BLOCK_SIZE/GROUP_SIZE/num_stages for the same fp8 GEMM.
