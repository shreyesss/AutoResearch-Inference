Background
On SM90,
fmaxf(x, 0)
compiles to a single FMNMX, which issues at 64 ops/clk/SM — half the rate of FADD/FFMA (128 ops/clk/SM). Meanwhile

compiles to a single FADD with the absolute-value operand modifier, which is a free encoding bit in the instruction word:
FADD R3, R3, |R3|    ; one instruction, full rate (cuobjdump, sm_90a)

score element in the MQA logits epilogue, so at 64 vs 128 ops/clk/SM the FMNMX form doubles the FP32 pipe time spent on relu alone.
