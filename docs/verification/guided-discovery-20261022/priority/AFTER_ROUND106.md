# Investigation priorities after round106

This is an independent qualitative allocation of research effort, not a gain ranking, new selection event, numerical ROI or modification of any original identity/baseline/status. The objective remains measured long-output ITL/goodput on the exact planned Flash1017/c455 configuration. Its live feature/backend/workload exposure remains unattested. No GPU, new broad simulation, live query or canonical mutation was performed.

## Recommended order is conditional, not a performance forecast

### 1. Keep FV86 and PJ01 as the main bounded operation-removal investigations

**FV86** identifies vocabulary residual work that can be omitted when the unchanged terminal full-prefix test succeeds. The dependency argument is concrete, and it leaves the native probability law, target logits and budget policy intact. Its opportunity is conditional: short/partial blocks may remove nothing; the residual launch, normalization and other stages remain. Round94 shows existing compatible counters can give useful lower/upper bounds before demanding a new joint trace, but neither those formulas nor accepted length supplies exposed time. A zero or very small upper opportunity bound under verified semantics can stop this work cheaply; unknown counter eligibility cannot be relabeled zero.

The most useful remaining CPU/source task is the **exact fused-flag adapter**, now delegated as round110: pick scratch ownership and fresh-writer coverage across AV/padding/reorder/zero-draft/greedy/placeholder paths, enumerate skipped consumers and retained stores, and determine the existing-launch insertion point. This closes an implementation contract. Another source-inspired latest-pass toy, broad sampler query or proof replay would not. A small explicit flag buffer is acceptable if its lifetime is simpler; zero allocation is not a new acceptance criterion. Compiled stored-ratio/RNG/branch parity remains a later native gate.

**PJ01** removes computed context-Q columns that the context-only caller discards. This is less dependent on rare acceptance events than FV86, provided the actual prepared backend/layout is eligible. It still requires a smaller-GEMM adapter and full context/query separation. Its logical output-width reduction is not its ITL reduction: input quantization, launch/norm/cache work, backend tactics and fixed overhead remain. Round48's shared-input quantization is a separate optional arm; do not combine its operation count with KV-only projection as a measured saving. Round67's existing capture path narrows placement, not external-backend capture/numerical proof.

Changed draft GEMM arithmetic does not automatically invalidate target sampling if every downstream verifier uses the actual resulting proposal law. Compare retained KV/cache behavior diagnostically and record proposal/confidence/AV/progress changes; do not impose universal draft bit-identity or attribute all effects to cheaper GEMM. Conversely, do not call a changed draft path a purely execution-only speedup if those effects are unmeasured.

**There is no justified total ordering between FV86 and PJ01 on expected gain.** Choose using the first compatible retained activation/backend/counter evidence. Both have a concrete missing operation and a bounded falsifier; neither has a measured deployment win.

### 2. Treat AV68 as a narrow conditional fix, not the centerpiece of another literature campaign

AV68 skips two same-step device publications overwritten before their consumers, under exactly the effective AV-plus-scheduled-drafts branch. It adds no cross-step cache/dirty state. That is a simpler integration contract than HM65's lifetime and writer invalidation. Keep CPU arrays, compaction, replacement writes and full query padding.

Its byte volume is small; possible value is avoided host/helper/copy-submission work, and that may be hidden. Long outputs alone do not prove host criticality. Effective AV may be inactive. Do not enable AV merely to make the experiment eligible or treat AV tuning as part of the same intervention. Once the gate is established, this is a reasonable early future implementation comparison because the change is easy to isolate, not because it is known to save more ITL than PJ01/FV86. Further generic host-overhead searching currently adds little.

### 3. Keep SL97 as a local kernel-layout experiment with a weaker performance premise

SL97 is **not** a broad scheduler/verifier integration change. It is local to coefficient layout, but it is not demonstrated operation-count elimination either. Native and donor each have four source-level exchanges per normalization stage; the donor has additional entry gathers and replicated registers. The round104 exit transport resolves ownership symbolically and adds a shuffle/selection/liveness cost. Any benefit comes from dependency scheduling/ILP/compiler behavior, which source counting does not resolve.

Small-token FULL overlap can select TileLang instead of this Mega coefficient route; do not disable useful overlap to fabricate exposure. A shape-route/exposure record can therefore decide priority before coding. If eligible, the next discriminators are compiled registers/spills and same-native-input coefficient/caller behavior, then exposed timing. Those need later authorized execution. Another lane-tag fixture now has little decision value. Preserve target primitives/epsilon/reduction grouping for the proposed selective arm; a deliberately changed target arithmetic policy would be a separate admitted experiment.

### 4. Retain SN98 as the larger scientific bet, with a clear stopping boundary

SN98 may remove ratio/residual statistics and optional proposal-cache traffic, but it adds target full-vocabulary noise sampling and can reduce committed progress. The full target/draft vocabulary heads and Markov logits remain. Its gain denominator is complete round cost per committed output under the unchanged EOS/length policy; neither removed arrays nor a paper acceptance objective supplies it.

The position-tape argument and existing donor triangulation make this a concrete algorithmic direction, not a vague shortcut. Round103 narrows the adapter, especially effective seed persistence, device AV boundaries, shifted candidate rows and unchanged output/cache counts. Standard unseeded serving is not admitted by an explicitly seeded parity arm: preemption/re-add currently can refresh the effective seed. Late noise-dependent fallback must retain the same target tape or have its own correct conditional argument. A batch-wide fallback caused by a newly unsupported request is not automatically safe.

More finite pathwise examples do not establish native PRF/processor/cache behavior or progress. Keep the initial explicitly seeded parity design small; do not spend on unseeded/mixed-feature deployment before that gate. Compare against the strong native block baseline and FV86 where implemented, not an obsolete unfused sampler. The old fixed-block evaluator cannot silently estimate this different verification law. This is a separate research lane, not a reason to delay all narrower fixes.

### 5. Park recent105/106 components until their specific exposure or adapter premise changes

**105 restoration-budget/cache reservation** is not useless because it is a policy. It needs actual costly restoration/preemption and meaningful policy groups under the intended deployment. Source availability and multi-tenant paper delay bounds do not establish either. Historical runs with other source pins/configurations, cache-hit reporting or absent metrics cannot certify current target exposure or non-exposure. A compatible retained event/metric artifact is more informative than another scheduler-paper query. If no relevant costly restoration occurs under verified observations, its proposed saving mechanism is absent there; missing observations leave it unknown.

**106 bulk combine reduction** is a selectively reusable component, but the target already has TMA movement and two-stage combine loads. Its dispatch has one chunk while combine has two; conflating those paths invents an opportunity. Replacing ordered FP32 register accumulation with a bulk-add destination needs a concrete format/initialization/order/completion contract and counts new passes. BF16/order scalar witnesses refute automatic arithmetic equivalence, not actual quality or every tolerance-controlled alternative. Donor atomic-finalizer comparisons and small-shape point estimates do not make this a priority over known discarded work. Preserve the option; reopen when a legal bounded adapter and exposed reduction/resource bottleneck can be named.

## Interactions and stop rules

- FV86 and SN98 both attack verifier work. SN98 replaces the law that makes FV86's terminal predicate applicable; their source byte savings cannot be added. A comparison against FV86 is useful; stacking their incompatible predicates is not assumed.
- PJ01/quantization reuse reduce context work; CF59 overlaps that work with sampling. Reducing either side changes the overlap denominator. Compare their combined interval with a strong control.
- AV68 preserves the existing AV policy. FV86 opportunity depends on actual admission/validity. PC76 or SN98 can change confidence/progress and must not silently inherit old opportunity counts.
- SL97 touches target coefficient computation, PJ01 draft computation, and SN98 verifier/RNG semantics. They require different correctness admissions; a universal bitwise rule or universal distribution-only rule is inappropriate.
- Stop repeating CPU fixtures after their source question is answered. Reopen on a named artifact, actual eligible route, source adapter closure or separately authorized native correctness/cost result. Unknown ROI is not a rejection, but more manifest or test-case volume is not stronger gain evidence.

No candidate is promoted or removed from memory by this note. Concrete next assignment: round110 FV86 flag design; keep existing source-only retention and scientific reviews independent of its outcome.
