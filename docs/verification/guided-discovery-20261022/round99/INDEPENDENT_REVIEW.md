No blocker. The frozen four-file packet accurately falsifies unconditional safe replacement of already-inspected randomness. An independent total-probability calculation gives P(output=0)=1/2+(a0-a1)/4, where a0,a1 are deterministic admission decisions for U=0,1. This matches all four reported policies and their complete four-outcome tables. An independent gate mixes fair conditional outputs and retains probability1/2. The keep-U arm is fair by construction because its output is identically the referenceU. This is a legitimate constructive countercontrol, not evidence of a successfully implemented persistent-noise engine. The mathematical test establishes that some correlated stopping/replacement rules are biased. It does not establish that every correlated policy is biased, that current DSpark AV uses this rule, or that native corrected rejection sampling is wrong. A valid conditional correction or a proved independent stopping rule can also preserve the target law.

Stable request/absolute-position noise is a candidate invariant, not a complete Gumbel proof. A native shared-noise integration must establish what its scheduler observes, noise-domain independence, salts/stream ownership, uncommitted lookahead lifetime and mapping through rollback, reallocation, bonus and EOS. Reusing a slot or round-indexed buffer is not automatically preserving the same output-position noise. Target numerical/logit-processor semantics also matter.

A pathwise target-only oracle is useful for a design that claims that coupling, not a universal same-seed-output requirement for all lossless stochastic decoders. Distributional correctness can hold with a different coupling. Conversely accepted length and even same-seed toy agreement cannot replace native law/implementation validation. The report's request to match source/RNG contract and its no-native-failure/no-continuous-proof/no-performance limits are appropriate.

The independent check read frozen outputs and used a separate Fraction formula; it did not execute the author script, rerun model sampling or inspect native AV for this packet.
