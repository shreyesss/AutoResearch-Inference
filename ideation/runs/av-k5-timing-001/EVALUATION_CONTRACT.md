A promising inventory can justify designing a subsequent bounded AV qualification. It cannot establish AV speedup: AV retains k5 proposal work, adds controller/compaction work, changes target graph behavior, and its nominal budgets can be consumed by DP synchronization and graph rounding. No latency/SLO qualification or remaining full hypothesis attempt is consumed by interpreting this diagnostic as a benchmark.

## Attribution and denominator rules

The parser must bind each trace to replica, internal DP rank, worker PID, physical GPU UUID, episode and source/runtime receipt. Require both episodes on all eight ranks. Match CUDA activities only through actual unambiguous runtime/driver correlation or CUDA graph launch association to the named CPU scopes. Graph replay needs evidence from the trace; kernel-name guesses, temporal proximity alone and treating everything outside target as draft are prohibited. Retain missing, ambiguous and unclassified activities.

For each device and episode, calculate interval unions in its own clock domain, including separate kernel, memcpy/memset and unknown categories. Target and draft proposal are mutually exclusive ownership categories; draft execution is a **subset** of draft proposal, not another component to add. Report their intersection and outside-proposal work explicitly. CPU range duration, GPU interval union, first-to-last stage span and complete-cycle wall span are different columns. Subtract only interval sets in the same domain; never subtract CPU totals from GPU totals. Do not sum overlapping kernels, sum eight GPU durations into wall time, or call unions FLOPs.

Use native context/generation counts to identify completed decode-only cycles. Report mixed/prefill cycles separately and retain their counts. Join an observed target step to its observed draft proposal and following target step on the same worker; discard partial boundary cycles from duration summaries but count them as exclusions. Define the complete GPU-cycle interval from the first correlated GPU activity of the current target to the first correlated GPU activity of the following target, on the same device. Require both boundaries and increasing timestamps. Clip **all** device activity/category intervals into this half-open interval; report target/draft/other/unattributed unions, target/draft overlap, all-busy union and remaining idle time. Keep CPU boundaries separate. This observed cycle partition is not a claim about a critical dependency chain. Each rank/episode needs at least **10 complete eligible decode cycles** for a qualified descriptive estimate. If identities, phase boundaries or complete-cycle joins are unavailable, report the limitation; no automatic extension.

For each eligible cycle retain available request/token counts, execution mode, target/draft graph descriptors if actually observed, category unions/spans and overlaps. Do not manufacture padded descriptors from native request counts. New ranges do not themselves expose all DP logical/padded metadata. Quantiles and medians summarize this bounded sample, without confidence intervals or claims of steady-state representativeness.

Cross-rank starts are not synchronized by an HTTP call. Compare per-rank distributions and report skew; do not align ranks merely by ordinal step or filename. A replica-level critical path requires a demonstrated shared step identity plus stream/event/collective dependencies and comparable time bases. Collective duration includes waiting and cannot be credited entirely to compute or communication savings. No summing participant durations or subtracting an assumed arrival skew.

Call a component **exposed** only when the observed stream/event dependency chain to completion or the next useful step establishes that its elapsed segment is on the relevant path and not hidden behind independent work. Same-stream order and source review can support a rank-local chain; that is not automatically the slowest DP path or client critical path. If graph dependencies or cross-rank alignment are absent, the study may establish occupied GPU time and overlap only; exposed service time remains unknown. Do not silently relabel exclusive occupancy as exposed delay.

## Validity and decision

Keep the existing replay fidelity limits as a diagnostic workload check: complete schedule986, at least99.5% completed, error fraction <=0.1%, profiling replay p99 <=500ms and replay-degraded false. At986 attempts the error-fraction condition requires zero errors. Preserve actual warmup outcomes and distinguish known warmup-warning fields from final profiling metrics. A profiler-induced violation is a measurement limitation, not a reason to relax the limit or claim source failure. Service SLO eligibility is irrelevant to whether the timing sample is readable and remains unqualified.

Timing-qualified requires: source/runtime/phase identity valid; all16 traces complete enough for the minimum cycles; required target and draft graph launches/activities unambiguously attributable; no dropped-activity/truncation warning affecting required scope; and at least95% of observed device-busy union assigned to the declared categories or explicitly identified other work. The remaining <=5% permits miscellaneous unclassified work, **not** missing target/draft graph attribution. Report every exclusion and the exact observed denominator. If traces fail these conditions, outcome is `instrumentation_inconclusive`, with raw traces retained. No profiler backend fallback or added synchronization is authorized by this contract.

For limited-budget prioritization, use20% as a **predeclared conditional cost-materiality heuristic**, not a speedup prediction. Within each rank/episode, divide the sum of target-attributed occupied unions in its nonoverlapping complete GPU-cycle intervals by the sum of those cycle spans. A `conditional_target_cost_material` result requires valid timing and this fraction >=20% on **every rank in both episodes**, with overlap, idle and request/shape context reported. This means target execution occupies enough of the sampled device timeline to justify designing a subsequent bounded AV cost/correctness qualification; it does **not** establish that its cost is exposed to clients or removable by AV. The original draft of this contract required an identified limiting DP critical path for a favorable decision; that was corrected **before any traffic** because the approved minimal scopes cannot establish that path. No additional runtime instrumentation is required for this achievable, narrower question.

If all qualified rank/episode fractions are below20%, the limited-budget result is `do_not_spend_next_attempt_on_AV_from_this_evidence`, not falsification of AV. Mixed fractions or inadequate attribution remain inconclusive; no selecting a favorable rank/window. Regardless of conditional materiality, exposed service savings remain unknown unless the separate dependency standard above is met. Target graph activity can include communication/waiting and cannot all be credited to reducible model computation.

Even a go does not authorize a claimed gain or automatic AV-on launch. A subsequent contract must measure AV eligibility, correctness, actual reduced target execution after DP/bucket rounding, controller overhead and accepted-token progress. Fixed-k5 acceptance alone cannot supply those costs. The earlier hard claim that DeepSeek k3 is blocked by checkpoint width5 is withdrawn: inspected source uses that width as a default, and the equality assertion is Qwen3-Omni-specific. DeepSeek k3 remains practically unqualified, not proven forbidden; it is outside this run.
