# H16 — Matched SGLang control and isolated group32 tuning

Research priority: 16. Root disposition: **defer implementation; retain conditional design**. Frozen-policy selection: **deferred**, score **68.0/100**. Scores are judgments, not speedups.

Original proposal: `h200-transfer-20261020-02`. Latest immutable identity: `hypothesis:08bd87539ae26b8989f5ff52cee80060d1e5d457f309d46f84618ba625b983ca`.

## Source observation

SGLang #41251 reports a multi-change H200 bundle under materially different engine/topology/precision settings. #40417 also supplies backend near-parity counterevidence.

## Upstream fix

Build a matched engine control if parity is feasible, then isolate the relevant GEMM tuning within SGLang before considering transfer.

## Root transfer prediction (untested)

A reproducible service gap could identify worthwhile components; it would not establish which bundled mechanism caused the gap.

## Baseline eligibility and cheapest first step

Match model/tokenizer revision, TP1/DP4/EP4, host Engram, FP8 KV, graph settings and real DSpark rejection semantics. Report any irreducible mismatch.

## Strongest comparator

Pinned vLLM versus pinned matched SGLang, followed by a single SGLang tuning change; do not call an unmatched recipe a kernel ablation.

## Activation denominator

Backend/shape/format coverage, target/drafter semantic parity and exact workload completion.

## Mechanism and competing explanation

Per-component critical path plus service frontier, workload mix and per-engine overhead.

## Reject, stop or defer

Parity is unavailable, quality differs, only old-engine comparison establishes gain, or tuning is inactive for our geometry.

## Correctness scope

Tokenizer/tool/reasoning encoding, stochastic sampling, quality, KV semantics and liveness across engines.

## Workload and held-out conditions

Same E1/E2 input bytes/arrival schedule and held-out workload; engine comparison remains a separate lane.

## Decision rules and cost

Keep applicability, activation, correctness, mechanism, service value and integration verdicts separate. Missing telemetry or intervals wider than the decision margin are inconclusive; an inactive branch is not a disproved algorithm. Freeze adequate exposure and correctness tolerances before GPU work. Use the protocol’s provisional 2% service-improvement / 3% secondary-regression pilot guard only when explicitly chosen; production SLO and node price remain unset. A component gain alone does not qualify service promotion.

Initial budget: at most two engineer-hours for source/build eligibility, then at most 20 minutes of component/activation GPU measurement if prerequisites pass. Stop on incompatibility or correctness failure. Any confirmation is a separate allocation: at least five paired full-run blocks plus held-out workloads, budgeted from their fixed durations. No automatic indefinite tuning or fleet-cost estimate. These are future test limits; no such work ran here.

## Model statement and experiment

Evaluate a workload-matched SGLang control, then isolate its H200 group32 GEMM configuration tuning as the first optimization arm. The supplied #41251 reports substantial H200 improvements from a multi-change SGLang bundle, but compares against SGLang main under TP8/EP1, another checkpoint, disabled host Engram, and bounded replay. Those results cannot predict an engine replacement here. First establish whether SGLang can preserve the target model revision, two independent TP1/DP4/EP4 replicas, Marlin recipe, host Engram, prefix behavior, and real DSpark policy. If it can, compare matched engines and separately enable only applicable group32 tuning configurations. Hold weight expansion, compensated projections, communication, and other fusions fixed. The hypothesis is that better launch configurations reduce exposed GEMM time on actual target shapes; the bundle's contribution from this mechanism is unknown.

**Control:** A: supplied pinned vLLM baseline, including Marlin selection subject to all-rank verification. S0: pinned SGLang with the same model/tokenizer semantics, two TP1/DP4/EP4 replicas/API2-equivalent frontend capacity, seq64, token/context limits, FP8 KV/block64, host Engram across DP, prefix caching, DSpark k5 probabilistic/block rejection, adaptive OFF, no bounded replay, and equivalent telemetry. Strong static comparator: best existing fixed eligible GEMM configuration selected on a disjoint calibration set.

**Correctness:** Run applicable G01–G05 once their definitions are available. Check tokenizer/chat/tool/reasoning behavior, stop conditions, prefix reuse, FP8 KV page handling, DSpark rejection and rollback, graph/eager parity, and all-rank collective participation. Verify tuning preserves quantization boundaries and assess numerical/output differences on paired quality tasks with truncations recorded. Aggregate equal scores or nonsignificant differences do not establish equivalence. The reported multi-node startup deadlock warrants targeted checks, not an assumed failure of this independent-EP4 layout.

**Cost:** Source/configuration parity checking is the inexpensive first gate. A second engine environment and configuration ablation add integration cost of unknown size. Startup and steady-state costs must be reported separately. Use P8*T/(3600*N_out), with node price P8 unset, plus a qualified-output denominator.

**Held out condition:** Reserve distinct agent sessions and Mooncake arrival traces. Evaluate long-context, ragged mixed prefill/decode, and cold/warm prefix conditions separately; retain cache-isolated 1K/1K as a diagnostic. Keep topology changes and seq128 outside this arm.

**Inconclusive if:** Parity, final-rank attribution, kernel dispatch, or speculation counters are unverified; other bundle changes cannot be isolated; intervals are too wide; or performance depends on unreported output-length, cache, or memory differences.

**Metrics:** All-rank tuned-kernel hits divided by eligible group32 GEMM calls, with M/N/K, selected configuration, phase, and graph coverage. Record request TPOT=(last-first token time)/(server output tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact server input/output tokens, node throughput, qualified goodput, replay lag, draft/verify/accepted deltas, prefix-hit tokens by final rank, KV blocks, preemption, peak memory, and errors. Separate profiled GEMM attribution from unprofiled serving results.

**Rejects if:** Inspection finds no applicable untuned shapes or no useful configuration delta; matching requirements are demonstrably unsupported without a credible remedy; or adequately precise serving results rule out practical improvement or establish regression. A tuning win that still loses to vLLM is not a deployment win.

**Supports if:** S1 activates on relevant shapes, passes correctness, and beats the strongest static S0 configuration by the preregistered useful-gain margin with corresponding exposed GEMM savings. Deployment improvement additionally requires S1 to beat A on a primary workload under matched semantics and quality/latency guardrails.

**Treatment:** First inspect whether exact deployment semantics, target shapes, and tuning-only controls exist. Establish S0 readiness and correctness before timing. S1 changes only applicable group32 configuration entries. Measure A versus S0 as an engine comparison, S0 versus S1 as the tuning attribution, and A versus S1 for deployment value. Use attestation, activation, correctness, unprofiled native-workload screening, separate mechanism profiling, then at least five alternating paired full-run confirmation blocks for small gains, with drift checks and full-run intervals.

## Critique retained

- h200_applicability_and_correctness_required: H200 execution is author-reported, but under TP8/EP1 and materially different checkpoint, Engram and replay settings. No pinned matched SGLang build or implementation evidence establishes the required TP1/DP4/EP4, host Engram, FP8 KV and real DSpark semantics together. The parity checklist is sound; whether its requirements can be met remains unknown.
- Priority below this task's threshold; reconsider with evidence or a revised policy
- The H200 bundle comparison does not identify which configurations changed, which target shapes would select them, or how much benefit survives without weight expansion and other fusions.
- S0 has no concrete revision or verified configuration-only ablation in the supplied packet. Merely naming a future pinned control does not establish readiness.
- SGLang #40417 reports that a seemingly better shape estimate regressed serving. It concerns a different Humming path and cannot veto dense-GEMM tuning, but it reinforces the need to validate actual lookup keys, routing and graph-tier behavior.
- The multi-node deadlock in #40744 motivates collective-participation checks; it does not establish failure of two independent EP4 replicas.
- If S0 beats vLLM while S1 adds nothing, the current combined support rule should not erase the independently useful engine result.

**Required revision:** Defer qualification: weighted score 68/100 and applicability unknown. Supply a concrete SGLang revision, a verified parity matrix and the exact configuration-only diff with dispatch consumers. Define the static comparator's search space and calibration budget.
