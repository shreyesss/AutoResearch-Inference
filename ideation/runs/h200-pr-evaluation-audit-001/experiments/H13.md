# H13 — Remove scheduler reservations for separate DSpark drafts

Lane: **main**. Live-layout priority: **13**. Status: conditional plan; no GPU test or measured gain.

## Problem and causal hypothesis

Scheduler accounting can charge K−1 draft slots even though DSpark drafts outside the target batch, reducing usable target tokens when the token budget binds.

## Applicability to our H200 deployment

Confirm MRV2 route, missing change and counters showing token budget prevents otherwise feasible work. At seq64, k5, the live16384 budget may make this irrelevant.

## Isolated control and treatment

A: current accounting. B: #59468, unchanged seq64, MBT16384 and k5. A later smaller-budget diagnostic is a separate config study.

## Evaluation and predicted mechanism

E1: native agent-f75; E2: frozen Mooncake median/burst/cache-heavy. H200 capacity discovery and held-out confirmation in PROTOCOL.md.

Measure: Scheduled target tokens versus charged tokens; budget-bound steps; active sequences; prefill/decode mix; E1/E2 goodput and tails.

## Why an additional evaluation is or is not needed

E1/E2 and upstream scheduler buffer-bound tests suffice. Do not lower MBT just to manufacture a production gain.

## Decision and falsification

Apply [the preregistered protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Confirm path and missing behavior before spending GPU time. Support requires correctness, predicted mechanism movement and a useful held-out SLO outcome. Reject an active, well-measured change with no useful endpoint gain or a correctness/tail regression. Mark absent-path, underpowered or failed-replay cases inapplicable/inconclusive; do not label them disproven. Correctness lanes establish only scoped correctness.

## Evidence limits

Author result at MBT512 is not transferable to16384. Defer if live scheduler never binds.
