What
Two fused paths for DeepSeek-V4.1, plus the MoE finalize plumbing they expose:
mHC sequence-parallel boundary
(
SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_MHC_SP_FUSION
, opt-in): inside the sharded region each sublayer boundary becomes one collective launch — reduce-scatter of the partial sublayer output, hc post + next combine + RMSNorm on this rank's rows, all-gather of the normalized next input — with the mixing statistics produced by dedicated CTAs of the same launch (NVLS from three peers up, peer load/store at two ranks). With decoder SWA bounded replay the SP region ends at the tail boundary instead of excluding SP outright.
Engram
: the hash tables shard on hash-column boundaries in symmetric memory, and the lookup defaults to an in-place gather straight into
wkv
's MXFP8 operand — no all-reduce, no dequant/requant round trip (a bf16-dequantizing gather covers
wkv
configurations without an MXFP8 input path, exactly; the shared host table is read the same way). The gate + residual update + the next boundary's combine/norm fuse into one seam kernel with SP / DP / TP variants (
SGLANG_OPT_DSV41_ENGRAM_TP_FUSION_MIN_TOKENS
gates the TP variant; the image-token mask rides the kernel as a per-row skip). CUDA only; ROCm and other platforms keep the existing paths.
MoE
: a raw
MoEOutput
's finalize now defers whenever the producer supports it and lands as one fused finalize + shared-add kernel writing the caller's buffer;
get_merged
honors
out=
on the unfused arm too, fixing a path where the sequence-parallel boundary read an unwritten buffer.
Accuracy
Kernel level: the direct gathers are bitwise-identical to the dequantizing reference (including e8m0 zero scales and fp8 denormal payloads) on both sm90 and sm100; the DP and SP seam variants are bitwise-identical to each other across row counts, ragged splits and skip masks, including NaN/Inf injection into masked rows; 200 back-to-back SP launches show stable semaphore rotation.
End to end (TP4/EP4, gsm8k 1319 questions, greedy): fused SP 0.9014 vs unfused 0.9113, McNemar p = 0.198 (not significant). A same-configuration rerun control scores 0.9113 / 0.9113 with only 36% exact-text agreement, i.e. the engine is not run-to-run stable at temperature 0 on this backend (as the cookbook documents), and the fused path's long-prompt logprob drift sits within that noise floor. TP-mode fusion scores 0.9052; TP2 (host table) 0.8954, all within the observed jitter band.
The seam consumes the previous boundary's lagged
pre
, so the fused math matches the unfused operator order; equivalence rather than bitwise identity is expected against the old path (the gate's sigmoid was already approximate there).
Notes
TP2 cannot hold the device tables beside the weights; the host table is the deployable form there (cookbook updated, along with the gather semantics).
Online weight updates remain rejected while derived hc weight splits are active; the probe follows the renamed stacked tensors.

Hi
@DarkSharpness
, I’m working on Engram WKV projection sharding in
#40508
, which is also tracked in the DSV4.1 roadmap
#42170
.
My approach partitions the WKV output dimension across TP ranks and gathers the projected activations before the existing gate. It reduces per-rank WKV weight storage as well as duplicated projection computation when token inputs are replicated.
I noticed this PR takes a different approach in the TP fusion path: partitioning token rows while keeping the full WKV weights. Could you take a look at
#40508
? I’d appreciate your thoughts on the tradeoff, particularly whether projection sharding could cover configurations where TP fusion is not selected. I’m happy to coordinate compatibility and validation between the two PRs.
