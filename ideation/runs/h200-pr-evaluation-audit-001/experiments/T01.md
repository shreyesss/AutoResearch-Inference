# T01 — TP4 alternative: remove Marlin expert padding

Lane: **different_topology**. Live-layout priority: **not ranked**. Status: conditional plan; no GPU test or measured gain.

## Problem and causal hypothesis

Pure TP4 shards expert width2304 to576; rounding to128 pads it to640, whereas64 alignment keeps576.

## Applicability to our H200 deployment

Current TP1/EP4 retains expert width2304, already divisible by128: no padding reduction in the requested live layout.

## Isolated control and treatment

Only in a separately qualified two-TP4 baseline, compare alignment128 versus64 for SM90 BF16 Marlin without A8.

## Evaluation and predicted mechanism

E1: native agent-f75; E2: frozen Mooncake median/burst/cache-heavy. H200 capacity discovery and held-out confirmation in PROTOCOL.md.

Measure: Padded weight bytes, allocated HBM, KV blocks, expert kernel time and goodput.

## Why an additional evaluation is or is not needed

Same E1/E2 plus kernel/loading tests after a separate TP4 baseline. Do not attribute a topology change to the PR.
