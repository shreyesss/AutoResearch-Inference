Under memory-constrained settings that rely on offloading, inference becomes I/O-bound due to CPU-GPU expert transfers, reducing TPOT. We introduce an inference-time expert prefetching scheme that speculates future expert selection from internal model representations, enabling expert transfers to overlap with computation and improving TPOT.
