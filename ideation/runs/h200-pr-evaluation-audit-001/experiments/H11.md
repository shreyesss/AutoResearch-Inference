# H11 — Coordinate DP prefill to protect decode progress

Lane: **main**. Live-layout priority: **11**. Status: conditional plan; no GPU test or measured gain.

## Problem and causal hypothesis

A prefill-heavy rank can delay synchronized decode peers. Bounded cross-DP coordination may improve decode tails while risking prefill starvation.

## Applicability to our H200 deployment

Observe mixed prefill/decode steps and peer waits first. Existing prefill interval controls must not overlap. All dummy, pause, cancellation and shutdown participants must remain synchronized.

## Isolated control and treatment

A: existing scheduler. B and C: separate #51199/#61834 proposals, only after protocol review. Train one bounded policy on discovery trace and freeze it for held-out windows.

## Evaluation and predicted mechanism

E1: native agent-f75; E2: frozen Mooncake median/burst/cache-heavy. H200 capacity discovery and held-out confirmation in PROTOCOL.md.

Measure: Per-rank backlog, oldest-prefill age, mixed-step time, accepted output tokens/step, collective wait, TTFT and ITL tails.

## Why an additional evaluation is or is not needed

Existing E1/E2 burst and cache-heavy suffice; add distributed protocol/deadlock fixtures, not a new load generator.

## Decision and falsification

Apply [the preregistered protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Confirm path and missing behavior before spending GPU time. Support requires correctness, predicted mechanism movement and a useful held-out SLO outcome. Reject an active, well-measured change with no useful endpoint gain or a correctness/tail regression. Mark absent-path, underpowered or failed-replay cases inapplicable/inconclusive; do not label them disproven. Correctness lanes establish only scoped correctness.

## Evidence limits

Inherited primary review. A decode gain accompanied by TTFT starvation is rejected; source step counts must be translated to variable DSpark accepted tokens.
