# H10 — Bounded coordinated prefill release

Research priority: 10. Root disposition: **defer implementation; retain conditional design**. Frozen-policy selection: **deferred**, score **72.0/100**. Scores are judgments, not speedups.

Original proposal: `h200-systems-004`. Latest immutable identity: `hypothesis:41df36bb4be53fef5cb6e5e98527dfdea3af1fe8535f5992a197b65ba82bd2df`.

## Source observation

#61834 coordinates prefill across DP ranks to reduce mixed-phase interference; its topology/workload results do not establish value on two local EP4 replicas.

## Upstream fix

Use per-step coordination; the root extension compares an overdue-request escape rule with simple static scheduling controls.

## Root transfer prediction (untested)

Aligned prefill could protect decode tails, but added waiting/communication may worsen TTFT and burst recovery.

## Baseline eligibility and cheapest first step

Inspect installed scheduler compatibility and exposed mixed-step interference; a per-step wall-clock check does not guarantee a hard waiting-time bound.

## Strongest comparator

Tuned existing scheduler, MBT and fixed prefill interval under the same budget before adaptive coordination.

## Activation denominator

Mixed-phase opportunities, coordination holds/releases/escapes and actual oldest-request wait; capture every rank.

## Mechanism and competing explanation

Decode stalls versus coordination time, prefill debt, queue tails and synchronized burst behavior.

## Reject, stop or defer

No mixed-step interference, simple tuning matches it, starvation/liveness failure, or TTFT regressions beyond predeclared bounds.

## Correctness scope

Fixed DP4/EP4 contract, idle/wake/cancel, no elastic/custom-scheduler assumptions, distributed agreement and bounded backlog.

## Workload and held-out conditions

Mixed E1 and burst E2; held-out prefill lengths and sustained overload.

## Model statement and experiment

Test coordinated prefill release only if measured mixed-step interference survives tuning the existing scheduler and static prefill interval. PR #61834 supplies a concrete per-step coordination protocol, but its reported interactivity gains come from an earlier MI355X branch and include substantial TTFT penalties; PR #51199 reinforces the need for a static comparator. Adapt the policy to each DP4 replica with MBT16384 and add an agreed wall-clock escape so protected decode periods cannot indefinitely postpone expensive chunks. Keep the CPU collective implementation fixed during this experiment. The prediction is fewer disruptive mixed steps, not an automatic throughput gain; promotion requires a better TTFT/TPOT frontier after accounting for queueing and coordination overhead.

**Control:** Attested baseline and the best prespecified static prefill interval at unchanged MBT16384. Also evaluate an explicitly separate scheduler/MBT tuning comparator. Keep CPU collective implementation, routing and draft accounting identical across policy arms.

**Correctness:** Test identical all-rank decisions, real/dummy/pause participation, chunk continuation, overdue release before protected-step rules, high-KV release, cancellation, wave reset and bounded progress. Ensure policy-off behavior is unchanged and both static interval and delayer cannot be enabled inconsistently. Check graph/KV state and output quality; obtain G01-G05 definitions before qualification.

**Cost:** Highest scheduler integration and validation cost in this shortlist; stop after static-policy screening if it dominates. No GPU execution performed. Cost remains C8*T/(3600*Nqualified), with unfinished work charged to elapsed time.

**Held out condition:** Primary agent-f75 8K/1K and fixed Mooncake anchors. Hold out long chunked prompts, correlated and uncorrelated bursts, a rank with no local decodes, high KV occupancy and repeated idle-to-active waves.

**Inconclusive if:** No agreed TTFT tradeoff exists, mixed-step exposure is insufficient, the branch remains unqualified, required counters are missing, or intervals leave a latency tradeoff unresolved. Provisional diagnostic SLOs cannot establish production suitability.

**Metrics:** Denominator: non-idle DP steps with pending prefill and active decodes; count eligible requests/chunks as well. Numerators: HOLD decisions, synchronized releases and avoided mixed steps. Record release reason, waiting age, per-rank actual prefill tokens, collective overhead, queue tails, TTFT tails, request TPOT, exact tokens, throughput, qualified goodput, KV/preemption, all-rank draft counters and replay lag.

**Rejects if:** Static tuning matches or dominates; purported gains depend on delayed or unfinished requests; TTFT/wait limits fail; added collective overhead cancels savings; or distributed progress fails.

**Supports if:** Compared with the strongest static policy, coordinated release reduces attributable mixed-step interference and improves the unprofiled serving frontier while satisfying preregistered TTFT and maximum-wait limits. Confirm small effects with at least five alternating paired full-run blocks, drift checks and held-out intervals.

**Treatment:** Inspect existing policy and exposure first. Qualify the adapted coordinated delayer, then compare at the same offered schedule and seq64. Do not combine it initially with h200-systems-002.

## Critique retained

- h200_applicability_and_correctness_required: The host protocol is plausibly adaptable to fixed DP4/EP4, but installed scheduler compatibility and the proposed overdue mechanism are unverified. The source excludes elastic EP and custom schedulers. A wall-clock flag checked once per step can override HOLD decisions; it does not by itself establish a hard request-wait bound during long steps, repeated chunks or resource starvation.
- Name DeepSeek-V4-Pro explicitly as the source model; the reported results do not measure V4.1-Flash.
- The wall-clock escape is a new adaptation whose progress semantics need specification.
- Earlier-branch results, cross-node comparisons and CPU simulations cannot establish the submitted branch's serving behavior.
- The treatment refers to h200-systems-002 rather than the current R2 identifier.

**Required revision:** Weighted score: 72/100; deferred on applicability. Define the overdue protocol and realistic wait bound, obtain implementation-level branch support, and preregister development-only tuning and TTFT limits. Correct the source-model attribution and R2 reference. Stop if static tuning dominates.
