# H16 — Reduce Hopper contiguous MQA epilogue cost

Lane: **main**. Live-layout priority: **16**. Status: conditional plan; no GPU test or measured gain.

## Problem and causal hypothesis

The SM90 contiguous MQA logits epilogue may use a costlier reduction/clamp sequence than needed. A revised epilogue could reduce prefill indexer work.

## Applicability to our H200 deployment

Resolve actual vendored library/header commit and prove the changed contiguous kernel is called by this model. Paged decode is not changed.

## Isolated control and treatment

A: exact vendored DeepGEMM. B: #452 at the same build options, isolated from other library updates.

## Evaluation and predicted mechanism

E1: native agent-f75; E2: frozen Mooncake median/burst/cache-heavy. H200 capacity discovery and held-out confirmation in PROTOCOL.md.

Measure: Contiguous MQA kernel time and launch shapes, prefill fraction, TTFT, indexer top-k agreement and output quality.

## Why an additional evaluation is or is not needed

E1/E2 prefill cohorts suffice for performance; upstream finite/edge numerical tests including overflow/NaN/ties are additional correctness coverage.

## Decision and falsification

Apply [the preregistered protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Confirm path and missing behavior before spending GPU time. Support requires correctness, predicted mechanism movement and a useful held-out SLO outcome. Reject an active, well-measured change with no useful endpoint gain or a correctness/tail regression. Mark absent-path, underpowered or failed-replay cases inapplicable/inconclusive; do not label them disproven. Correctness lanes establish only scoped correctness.

## Evidence limits

Distribution-package absence does not mean vendored DeepGEMM is absent. Do not claim decode gain from a prefill-only change.
