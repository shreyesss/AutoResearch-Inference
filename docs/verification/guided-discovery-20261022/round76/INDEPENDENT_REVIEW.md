## AV and fitting

The trained confidence head is parent-conditioned (`target-dspark.py:182–199`); it is not automatically recalibrated by changing a sampling scalar. The entropy-estimator path consumes temperature (`target-speculator.py:479–491`), while a present trained head disables that estimator, not AV. Preserve the actual primary AV policy/configuration, record its realized budgets and progress, and allow its decisions to respond to changed inputs. Holding the original calibration is not proof it remains reliable. Disabling or retraining/calibrating scheduling is a declared additional arm.

The paper's same-prefix conditional-independence window objective is not a native block-progress theorem. Common-prefix overlap can screen a scalar; changed parents require new conditional target evidence. Held-out request/session splits prevent leakage but do not fill missing counterfactual prefixes. Root's exact Fraction counterexample is useful algebraic evidence and was transparently shared with the author; it is neither a blinded reviewer test nor native block-rejection validation. The 11 AST checks establish source fragments, not numerical correctness.
