# C09 — Candidate-only long-context decode scoring

Status: **Proposed; untested**. Updated: 2026-10-21 (Asia/Calcutta).

**Claim:** Compare dense scoring with #62000 candidate-K gathering and #62463 direct paged-K scoring as separate alternatives, keeping selection semantics and prefill unchanged.

**Why:** Dense Hopper decode indexing can score the full compressed context even though only a candidate subset will be consumed.

**Research finding:** Both H200 audits converge. Fresh shows both patches conflict with installed code; #62000 reports a single-request regression. Neither author result proves current H200 value.

**Original design:** [consolidated family card](../runs/h200-cross-audit-comparison-001/families/C09.md). The original card is a pre-execution snapshot; current execution history is below.
