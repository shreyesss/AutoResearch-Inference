## Model statement and experiment

At a fixed, validated Humming GEMM selection, test per-token FP8 activations against BF16 while retaining native MXFP4 weights. This isolates activation traffic and arithmetic from backend selection. #59488 describes enforcing and logging the requested schema, while #60600 reports that installed Humming 0.1.16 rejects block-FP8/MXFP4 and can silently substitute per-token FP8 when fallback is allowed. Consequently, #61875's block-FP8 claim cannot justify enabling that format here. The transfer hypothesis is that per-token FP8 saves more expert work than quantization adds at the target's full-size EP4 experts. Establish named-format support or an equivalent verified configuration first; target quality, routed-MoE benefit, and effect size remain unknown.

**Control:** Validated Humming BF16 with a fixed GEMM choice, including indexed as the explicit simple comparator. Retain original MARLIN and the best validated static backend as deployment incumbents. Preserve all other supplied settings and the primary agent-f75/Mooncake workloads.

**Correctness:** Check scale dtype/granularity, quantization boundaries, clipping at swiglu_limit10, routing, numerical outliers, graph replay, and target/drafter inheritance. Use prespecified numerical tolerances and a quality noninferiority assessment. Acceptance is a diagnostic, not a correctness score. Apply relevant common TF32 checks.

**Cost:** Low configuration cost if supported; otherwise a small integration whose extent is unknown. Future cost uses P8*T_hours/N_valid_output_tokens for both replicas; H200 price and evaluation duration remain unknown.

**Held out condition:** Unseen long-context and reasoning/tool-use quality cases; low-concurrency decode; ragged and mixed arrivals; cold/warm prefixes. Keep cache-isolated 1K/1K results separate from primary workload evidence.

**Inconclusive if:** Requested and executed schemas cannot be distinguished, target/drafter coverage is incomplete, baseline precision changes, quality uncertainty is too large, or confidence intervals cannot distinguish a useful gain from regression.

**Metrics:** Count verified per-token-FP8 executions over eligible W1/W2 opportunities on all ranks, including target and drafter. Record quantization time, expert time, activation storage, and fallback counts. Measure request TPOT=(last-first)/(output_tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact throughput, goodput/replay lag, KV/preemption, draft/verify/accepted deltas, and quality.

**Rejects if:** Quality or numerical gates fail, quantization cost eliminates useful serving improvement with adequately precise measurements, or the incumbent remains superior across the intended primary operating range.

**Supports if:** Verified per-token FP8 reduces complete expert cost and improves a prespecified serving metric over fixed BF16 while satisfying numerical and quality guardrails. Deployment additionally requires beating the incumbent static backend. Confirm small gains with at least five alternating paired full-run blocks and drift-aware run-level intervals.

**Treatment:** First establish schema support without treating package presence as activation. Then verify actual FP8 inputs/scales, pass correctness, run an unprofiled precision screen, diagnose the complete expert boundary separately, and confirm paired serving results. If a source port is needed, run both precision arms on that same source build.

## Critique retained

- h200_applicability_and_correctness_required: Per-token FP8 has relevant H200 engineering evidence, and excluding block-FP8/MXFP8 addresses the supplied 0.1.16 restrictions. However, installed named-format plumbing, actual W1/W2 schemas and target/drafter inheritance remain unknown. Proposed scale, clipping, graph and quality checks are suitable but not completed evidence.
- The H200 dense probe supports testing per-token FP8, not predicting full-size EP4 MoE or serving improvement.
- The named activation option may require a port on the pinned branch; the existing Humming input-schema environment control deserves an explicit eligibility check first.
- A generic quality subset does not yet define how native agent behavior, truncation and long reasoning/tool-use failures will be scored.

**Required revision:** Deferred at 76/100 because applicability is unknown. Specify the cheapest verified configuration route before proposing a named-format port. Publish requested versus resolved W1/W2 schemas for target and drafter, and define numerical tolerances, quality margins and complete-boundary timing before future execution.
