## Source observation

Installed SM90 contiguous header still uses fmaxf and uncompensated scales. DeepGEMM #452 reports H200 kernel gains, with a best-of-three timing caveat and an overbroad numerical exactness claim.

## Upstream fix

Replace ReLU arithmetic with x+abs(x) and halve downstream scale factors in the contiguous kernel.

## Root transfer prediction (untested)

Fewer epilogue instructions may help exposed prefill scoring. Intermediate overflow/underflow and rounding require a defensible domain or a stock fallback.

## Critique retained

- h200_applicability_and_correctness_required: The matching SM90 header and author-reported H200 tests support a plausible narrow port. However, active artifact provenance and a sufficient numerical-domain invariant remain unknown. The author's "bit-exact numerics" assertion is not generally valid: intermediate doubling can overflow, scale halving can underflow, and exceptional-value behavior differs. The proposal correctly acknowledges these issues and specifies reference, top-k, graph/cache, distributed and quality checks, but their outcomes and applicable G01–G05 definitions are unavailable.
- An unchanged installed header does not prove the serving process executes it. Include resolution, loaded dependencies, JIT cache provenance and eligible invocation coverage must establish the effective baseline.
- Finite accumulator observations alone cannot qualify the arithmetic. Bounds must cover doubled accumulators, weighted products, intermediate reductions and halved scales under actual compiler rounding and flush-to-zero behavior. The scalar overflow counterexample challenges universal equivalence; it does not establish a failure on reachable deployment inputs.
- The highlighted 8K author benchmark uses BF16 logits. Supplied vLLM #61149 states "SM90 and SM120 keep the FP8 format, FP32 weights and FP32 logits." That is an attributed branch-description claim, not proof of pinned deployment membership, but it makes dtype and compressed-row API matching an explicit transfer question.
- Kernel throughput gains cannot establish primary-workload TTFT or goodput gains. Prefix reuse, other prefill stages and contention may remove the exposed benefit; the proposed controls appropriately test these alternatives.
- The experiment's decision thresholds and stopping budget remain unspecified. Five paired blocks are a minimum design requirement, not assurance that a small effect will be resolved.

**Required revision:** Retain as a deferred inspection candidate, not a qualified deployment selection. The weighted score is 78/100, but the unknown required applicability/correctness gate prevents qualification. Before a later bounded experiment, document the active artifact and shape/dtype/API mapping, establish an enforceable numerical eligibility domain or retain stock behavior, and predeclare correctness tolerances, practical-effect and regression thresholds, activation coverage and stopping limits. No demonstrated speedup is required before that experiment.
