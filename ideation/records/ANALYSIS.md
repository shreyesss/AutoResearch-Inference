## C25 component evidence and remaining inference boundary

The [overnight component review](../runs/h200-overnight-001/evaluator/C25_COMPONENT_RESULT_REVIEW.md) establishes a real local sampler improvement with installed RNG and exact cache/token semantics. Its 45–83µs savings at the historically common submitted capacities cannot be directly translated into ITL: call frequency, exposed stage time and queueing effects remain unmeasured. The historical capacity histogram includes warmup/profiling and is not a latency weighting. The next native pilot uses captured actual model activations but remains a padded dummy-stage proxy, with separate real request lifecycle checks. Neither stage is a service win.

C24's [joint dispatch reconstruction](../runs/h200-overnight-001/developer/C24_JOINT_DISPATCH.md) shows that the existing objective already includes draft cost, and independently chosen local verification budgets do not determine synchronized draft capacities. Additional remote confidence/budget state or a distinct post-sync policy must be designed before claiming a deployable coupled optimizer. C26 closes on actual BF16 allocation/load evidence; casting weights to activate an unrelated FP32 branch would change the question.
