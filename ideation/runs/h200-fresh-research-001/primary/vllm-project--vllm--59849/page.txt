Same fix as
#59848
, for DSv4.1. Default
cpu_offload
Engram tables use
pin_memory=True
, which rounds each table up to a power of two. Register them at exact size through the existing THP helper, without huge pages. Draft: GB10 only. cc

#59327
lookup bench on GB200?

's sort.
