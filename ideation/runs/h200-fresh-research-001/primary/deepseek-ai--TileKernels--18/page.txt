Hand-written CUDA kernels for the Manifold HyperConnection (MHC) pipeline, targeting NVIDIA H20-3e (SM 90) with bf16/f32 mixed precision.
Four fused kernels:
mhc_expand: [M,H] -> [M,4,H] with vectorized uint4 loads
mhc_pre_fused: GEMM + RMSNorm + Split + Sinkhorn + ApplyMix in one launch
mhc_post: residual update with per-token comb/post_mix
mhc_head: LM head preprocessing (thin GEMM + RMSNorm + sigmoid + mix)
All correctness tests pass against PyTorch reference. Build via torch.utils.cpp_extension, exposed through pybind11 bindings.
