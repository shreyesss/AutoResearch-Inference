Overview
The fused mHC post + pre-norm kernel runs twice per decoder layer in DeepSeek-V4/V4.1 and GLM-5.3-Flash. Its split configs were tuned on GB300 (
#56633
). This PR gives SM90 its own config for 1–8 tokens:
(tile_n, n_splits, n_thr) = (12, hidden / 512, 256)
. Other architectures and token counts are unchanged.
Claims
On H20 at 1–8 tokens, the op gets faster:
DeepSeek-V4-Flash and GLM-5.3-Flash (hidden 4096): 4.4–16.3%.
DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (hidden 5120): 9.1–14.1%.
DeepSeek-V4-Pro (hidden 7168): 2.1–22.8%.
The band includes DSpark k=5 decode at C1, which runs 6 tokens per step.
Residual streams are bit-identical. The mixing coefficients, and through them the layer inputs, differ only in FP32 summation order.
Startup warmup compiles the new variant. Off SM90, the set of compiled kernels is unchanged.

The minimum tensor traffic is 1.7–3.9 MB per op, corresponding to an effective bandwidth of 152–308 GB/s.
From 9 to 32 tokens, main and this PR pick the same config, and their times agree within ±1.1%.
At 5120, the new config had the lowest total time over 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8 tokens in a sweep of 63 configs. It was within 4.6% of the best config at each of those token counts.

Details
Why it is faster.
Every (token, tile, k-slice) block recomputes the post mapping.
main's
(2, 8, 128)
runs 12 tiles, and each thread makes 4–7 serial memory round trips.
The new config runs 2 tiles with 2 round trips each.
Band.
A separate candidate config,
(12, 8, 128)
, improved latency at 9–18 tokens on H20. Those gains may depend on H20's 78-SM grid scheduling and have not been validated on H100/H200, so this PR leaves that band unchanged.
H100/H200.
These GPUs are not measured. Both grids should fit in one wave on 132 SMs, so the gain is expected to carry over. The 6–7-token dip at 7168 looks like a wave spill on H20 and is not expected there.
Scope.
SM100, SM120 and ROCm are untouched. No open PR changes this config.
