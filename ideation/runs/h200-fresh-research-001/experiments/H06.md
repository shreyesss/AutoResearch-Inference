# H06 — Candidate gather versus direct paged indexer scoring

Research priority: 6. Root disposition: **planned conditional diagnostic; prerequisites unresolved**. Frozen-policy selection: **deferred**, score **72.0/100**. Scores are judgments, not speedups.

Original proposal: `h200-attn-20261020-02`. Latest immutable identity: `hypothesis:ebbeb3770815c1a4f41305f14c23ad88eb1d0b7ee21f60ff4115237924d0865f`.

## Source observation

#62000 and #62463 provide Hopper candidate-only implementations. The first reports single-request regression; the second has small samples and incomplete quality coverage. Both conflict with installed source.

## Upstream fix

Score only retained candidate blocks, either after KV gathering or directly through page tables; retain dense fallback.

## Root transfer prediction (untested)

Avoiding irrelevant scoring may win at long contexts, while gather, irregular access or candidate bookkeeping may dominate shorter contexts.

## Baseline eligibility and cheapest first step

Match FP8 cache layout, compressed row IDs, candidate selection semantics and dependencies to this model; preserve dense selection semantics before claiming a lossless port.

## Strongest comparator

Current dense scoring, gather variant, direct-paged variant as separate arms. Compare exact candidate sets and equivalent fallback rules.

## Activation denominator

Candidate-path calls / eligible indexer calls; candidate fraction, fallback reasons, live lengths and token rows.

## Mechanism and competing explanation

Scored KV bytes, gather traffic, launch count, logits allocation and exposed indexer time; check whether smaller candidate sets, rather than implementation, explain gains.

## Reject, stop or defer

No eligible long-context work, selection/quality failure, single-request/short-context regression beyond guard, or failed graph replay.

## Correctness scope

Causal bounds, invalid candidates, ties, physical remapping, changing lengths inside graphs, numerical logits/top-k and quality.

## Workload and held-out conditions

Long-context bands plus D1/E1/E2; held-out lengths and low concurrency, especially single request.

## Model statement and experiment

Test direct paged candidate-only indexer scoring as a conditional replacement for dense scoring followed by masking in consumer layers. The supplied #62463 patch describes reading candidate K in place and retaining device-resident live lengths; #62000 instead gathers K before scoring. These are competing implementations of the same work-elimination mechanism, not cumulative optimizations. H20 evidence is conflicting: gathering regressed at one request, while direct scoring's lower ITL came from tiny samples and did not consistently improve throughput. The transfer hypothesis is that avoiding both discarded scoring and intermediate K copies helps eligible H200 long-context decode. First establish actual indexer shapes, cache format and baseline dispatch. Keep dense scoring as the short-context comparator and default; require native 8K/1K evidence before claiming a useful change to this deployment.

**Control:** Pinned baseline dense scoring plus candidate masking and the unchanged deployed top-k backend. Use three mutually exclusive static arms: dense with both candidate flags off; #62000 gather only; #62463 direct paged only. Dense is the strongest simple short-context comparator. Calibrate and freeze any gather workload gates before held-out evaluation; FORCE is for validation, not a production policy.

**Treatment:** Start with source/configuration membership and eligibility checks. After activation and correctness, screen each arm unprofiled on native agent-f75 and fixed-schedule Mooncake at seq64, then run separate long-context and cache-isolated diagnostics. Compare surviving arms in at least five alternating paired full-run blocks for small gains. Keep topology, speculation, prefixes, logging and scheduler settings fixed. Do not add a host context-length branch inside captured execution; any future mixed-workload routing requires separate graph-safe qualification.

**Metrics:** Activation: candidate-path consumer calls and rows divided by all structurally eligible consumer calls and rows, stratified by live compressed length, row count, eager/graph mode and rank. Record fallback reasons and whether consumer mask kernels disappear. Measure request TPOT using exact server output tokens, TTFT tails, throughput, historical-scorer goodput, replay lag, all-rank draft/verify/accepted deltas, prefix hits, KV blocks, preemption and workspace high-water marks. Separate diagnostics measure the complete indexer path, not mismatched scoring buckets.

**Supports if:** All relevant ranks exercise the intended path; reference, graph and model-quality checks pass; complete-path time falls at matched inputs; and unprofiled paired intervals demonstrate useful TPOT or goodput improvement without unacceptable primary-workload or tail regressions. A long-context-only win supports only that scope, not deployment-wide promotion.

**Rejects if:** The feature already executes equivalently; required cache or shape contracts cannot be satisfied; numerical, causal or replay correctness fails without a credible repair; or adequately powered tests show no useful improvement over dense or the strongest eligible alternative. A native-workload regression rejects blanket activation even if a long-context diagnostic improves.

**Inconclusive if:** Only profiler-derived timings or streamed ITL improve; acceptance or prefix behavior cannot be joined to requests; eligible opportunity coverage is inadequate; the GPU correctness report is missing; or independent full-run intervals cannot distinguish benefit from regression.

**Held out condition:** Previously unseen long-context lengths, single-request decode, ragged mixtures spanning short and long contexts, cold/warm prefixes, page-table remapping, preemption and mixed prefill/decode. Determine feasible H200 concurrency from actual KV residency; do not import H20 capacity.

**Correctness:** Validate scores against an independent FP32 reference with justified tolerances, tie-aware selection against dense scoring, and a model-quality subset. Exercise invalid, duplicate and padded candidate handling; empty/partial contexts; request-local remapping; block64 layouts and strides; speculative causal bounds; and unchanged source-layer behavior. Capture short contexts and replay with changed lengths, pages and request assignments; compare eager results and check stable workspace allocation. Author-described tests are not evidence they passed on H200. Map to applicable G01-G05 when definitions are available.

**Cost:** Higher integration and verification cost than plan sharing: complete kernel/dispatch review, numerical validation and three-arm comparison are required. Gate expensive long-context tests on eligibility and the unprofiled screen. Measure workspace and KV-capacity effects rather than infer them. Report P*T/(3600*N_output), with unknown full-node hourly price P; also report qualified-token cost. Profiled runs are excluded from the serving frontier.
