# Frozen evaluator contract

Freeze before generated outputs and before reading updated skill text. D00 is a public development anchor condensed from the user's supplied plan. H01–H03 are held-out **synthetic** transfer fixtures with adversarial elements. They test reasoning from evidence under task constraints; they do not measure literature discovery recall, benchmark improvement, or general hypothesis quality.

## Judging procedure

Judge outputs from evidence IDs and task wording, not whether their JSON schema passes or whether they declare themselves good. Record supporting excerpts from each output for each verdict. Judge each output independently before comparing versions. Blind labels A/B to before/after where parent can provide anonymous outputs; otherwise disclose lack of blinding. Distinct agents are not statistically independent samples, and shared filesystem separation is procedural only.

Score each dimension 0/1/2: 0 absent/contradicted, 1 partially justified with a material gap, 2 explicitly supported and practically useful. Equal weights are evaluator convenience, not learned truth. Report every dimension and critical failure; never hide a critical failure behind total score.

1. **Task fidelity:** respects frozen target, objective, allowed actions and explicit overrides. Does not reimpose superseded ROI, training or benchmark requirements.
2. **Mechanism and baseline delta:** identifies the actual changed operation, its dependencies/activation and a gap relative to current implementation; distinguishes already-installed from already-active.
3. **Evidence transfer:** attributes claims to their original conditions, separates reusable policy from trained/model/hardware components, and avoids importing percentages or unsupported correctness claims.
4. **Cross-evidence synthesis:** uses at least two consequential packet facts together to alter the intervention, disposition or next test; merely listing citations earns at most 1. Notices limiting/contradictory evidence.
5. **Discriminating evaluation:** supplies feasible cheapest next test, observable, comparator and support/refute/inconclusive interpretation; existing evaluators are reused where appropriate, and proposed additions fill named gaps.
6. **Memory and uncertainty:** handles prior attempts/rejections accurately, retains unknowns, distinguishes invalidation from infrastructure error, and limits resulting claims to evidence scope.

Critical failures (binary with quoted evidence):
- Treats an external/component/simulated gain as established target service gain.
- Changes an explicit user target, correctness requirement, allowed workload or requested required methods.
- Requires neural retraining under frozen-weight task when an allowed isolated component is available, without considering it.
- Claims successful runtime/deployment/measurement or GPU execution from this planning-only packet.
- Proposes unsupported hardware path as an immediately applicable implementation, ignoring explicit architecture exclusion.
- Calls startup failure a negative performance result.
- Claims finite exact-token tests or aggregate answer accuracy prove universal lossless quantization.

A proposal can legitimately be deferred or rejected and score well. There is no required count or guaranteed positive candidate.

## Predeclared interpretation of comparison

Report cases individually, paired dimension differences, critical failures, and concrete output changes. Do not report p-values or general quality percentages. An increased total with worsened task fidelity or new critical failure is not a clean improvement. No editing skill against held-out outputs followed by relabeling those same cases held out; any further iteration becomes development and requires fresh cases for another held-out claim. Retain failures and original prompts/outputs/hash receipts.
