Update the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash "B200 AgentX tuning" guide with the settings qualified in [SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#3837](https://github.com/SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX/pull/3837) (head `d2d90439`).
