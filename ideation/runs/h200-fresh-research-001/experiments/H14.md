## Model statement and experiment

Test direct-NVLink DeepEP dispatch in two attributable stages: compatible backend activation, followed by the triple-buffered prefetch change in #693. First verify the effective baseline transport, four-rank NVLink connectivity, and whether the pinned engine can reach the elastic direct-NVLink implementation while preserving Marlin and its activation payload. The supplied patch overlaps preparation of the next token with outstanding remote stores, but its positive evidence is an eight-H100, remote-only bandwidth test. Four-rank serving may have more local traffic, smaller messages, and greater resource contention. Compare unmodified DeepEP with the baseline before comparing patched and unmodified DeepEP at fixed compute and numeric settings. Resolve the buffer-lifetime invariant and graph behavior before performance testing. Historical mixed-configuration regressions motivate these controls but do not establish that DeepEP will lose here.

**Control:** A: supplied pinned vLLM deployment with its effective transport verified, retaining Marlin, both TP1/DP4/EP4 replicas, API2, seq64, and all other baseline settings. B: compatible unmodified direct-NVLink DeepEP with the same expert computation and payload. Strong comparator: best legal static unmodified dispatch setting chosen on disjoint calibration traffic, plus an unmodified control matching the patch's dispatch geometry.

**Correctness:** Run relevant G01–G05 after obtaining their definitions. Verify dispatch/combine token identity, routing weights, scale metadata, duplicate destinations, zero-token ranks, ragged batches, buffer sizing/alignment, repeated wraparound, and maximum supported graph tiers. Establish the three-buffer/store-group lifetime invariant and host/device sizing agreement; stress graph capture/replay and collective ordering. Preserve DSpark rejection and KV rollback. If reduction order changes, assess numerical and paired quality effects. The closed Hopper issue reports a workaround removing DeepEP, not a demonstrated fix.

**Cost:** Compatibility inspection is cheap; an elastic adapter and buffer/graph validation may require substantial integration work, with effort unknown. Stop before GPU testing if no comparable path exists. No execution in this run. Parameterize full-node cost as P8*T/(3600*N_out), with P8 unknown, and report qualified-output cost separately.

**Held out condition:** Separate cold/warm 1K/1K diagnostics, long-context mixed prefill/decode, ragged arrivals, empty and uneven ranks, local-heavy routing, and skewed expert demand. Exercise both EP4 groups concurrently; keep seq128 separate.

**Inconclusive if:** The engine reaches another DeepEP implementation, telemetry or remote-traffic coverage is inadequate, the buffer invariant is unresolved, compute/format changes remain confounded, or only remote-only bandwidth improves. Wide intervals and substantial baseline drift also remain inconclusive.

**Metrics:** All-rank direct-NVLink and triple-buffer path hits divided by eligible MoE dispatch invocations, with remote routed rows/bytes as additional exposure denominators. Record local traffic fraction, per-rank expert counts, message sizes, dispatch/combine time, exposed critical-path time, shared memory, occupancy, and graph fallbacks. Serving metrics: request TPOT=(last-first token time)/(server output tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact tokens, throughput, qualified goodput, replay lag, draft/verify/accepted deltas, KV blocks, preemption, and failures.

**Rejects if:** Inspection establishes no useful delta, incompatible transport/compute contracts lack a credible remedy, synchronization correctness fails, or adequately precise primary-workload comparisons rule out practical improvement. A patch that improves B but still loses to A is not a baseline deployment improvement.

**Supports if:** C beats the strongest unmodified DeepEP comparator with verified pipeline activation and reduced exposed dispatch time; and C also improves over A on a predeclared primary workload beyond the useful-gain margin without quality or latency guardrail violations. Report backend activation and patch effects separately.
