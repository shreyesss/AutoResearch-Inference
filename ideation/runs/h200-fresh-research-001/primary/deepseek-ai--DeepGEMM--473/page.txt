2. Two key optimizations
Small M: tiled register decoding + RS WGMMA.
Pack each 256-row x K128 weight stage into a contiguous 16 KiB tile and decode directly into register A fragments, avoiding expanded-weight shared-memory traffic and associated synchronization. Extend
feat: add SM90 FP8 x MXFP4 MegaMoE support
#411
's swapAB with N8/16/24/32/40/48 buckets, selected double buffering, and direct SwiGLU/quantization epilogues.
Large M and tails: MXFP4 split-M.
Use 128x256 tiles with two math warpgroups sharing decoded weights; specialize partial tiles with RS buckets, a dual N96 swap path, and empty-half WGMMA skipping. Order dynamic tails by valid rows and preserve the scaling chain for bitwise repeatability.

Validation and reproduction
Correctness:
single-GPU/EP8 reference and repeatability checks pass (MXFP4 tolerance 0.002; FP8 0.01); 96 per-rank outputs match the accepted pre-port implementation bitwise.
SASS:
six FP8 checks unchanged; 30 measured MXFP4 cubins have STACK <=16 B and no CALL.ABS.
Reproduction:
frozen commits, commands, maximum-rank timings, and per-round data are in
docs/sm90-fused-mxfp4-benchmarks.md
and
docs/benchmarks/sm90-fused-mxfp4-h20.csv
