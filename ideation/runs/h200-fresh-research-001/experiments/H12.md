# H12 — Native-MXFP4 SM90 MegaMoE adapter

Research priority: 12. Root disposition: **defer implementation; retain conditional design**. Frozen-policy selection: **deferred**, score **63.0/100**. Scores are judgments, not speedups.

Original proposal: `H200-COMP-005`. Latest immutable identity: `hypothesis:36df071dcbdc77a9b52132ab4f9eb7acd737660898bf49542c71d2f9b11caf78`.

## Source observation

FlashInfer #6343 includes EP4 and native MXFP4, but benchmark model dimensions differ. #6517 repairs stale workspace and documents scale conversion; its H200 push tuning is EP8-only.

## Upstream fix

Build a framework adapter for the native FP4 API, weight transformation, collective symmetric workspace and execution plans; #62603 is not that native-FP4 adapter.

## Root transfer prediction (untested)

Fusing dispatch/expert computation/reduction may reduce communication and staging cost at eligible shapes, after accounting for packing and workspace.

## Baseline eligibility and cheapest first step

Check E384/H5120/I2304/top6,96 local experts, clamp10, weight byte order, scale ranges, group semantics and installed FlashInfer0.7.0 API. Alignment alone is insufficient.

## Strongest comparator

Current Marlin+AG/RS and qualified Humming at identical checkpoint/routing; do not combine backend gain with an EP8 topology change.

## Activation denominator

Native FP4 plan calls / eligible MoE calls, actual selected plans and all-rank workspace usage.

## Mechanism and competing explanation

Include staging/packing/dispatch/combine, graph lifetime and HBM/KV loss; published kernel tables exclude some preparation.

## Reject, stop or defer

No exact-shape/clamp/layout support, stale hot-expert workspace, scale saturation, or workspace/precision cost removes service gain.

## Correctness scope

Weight conversion versus FP32 reference, hot/empty experts, repeated launches/graphs, collective ordering, all-rank errors and quality.

## Workload and held-out conditions

Exact-shape component matrix before E1/E2; held-out expert skew and token counts.

Keep applicability, activation, correctness, mechanism, service value and integration verdicts separate. ## Model statement and experiment

Investigate an opt-in native-MXFP4 FlashInfer MegaMoE adapter for each EP4 replica, with an eligibility check before implementation. #6517 describes SM90 FP4 kernels, checkpoint-layout requirements, and a workspace cleanup repair; its H200 push gains concern EP8 plans and do not transfer here. #62603 supplies complementary evidence that fused dispatch, expert computation, and combine can help eligible FP8 workloads, but it is not a native-MXFP4 V4.1 integration. The transfer hypothesis is that fusion reduces exposed communication and staging relative to the best validated static backend. Require exact E384/H5120/I2304/top6 support, swiglu_limit10, legal EP4 plans, and globally consistent workspace sizing. If these cannot be established, defer the port; do not substitute converted FP8 weights or an unclamped activation.

**Control:** Best validated static MARLIN or Humming configuration, including indexed and supported precision comparators, under the supplied topology and primary agent-f75/Mooncake workloads. Use a dependency-matched non-fused control if the adapter requires an upgrade.

**Correctness:** Validate nibble order, decoder scale ranges and per-expert global-scale redistribution against independent native-weight references. Preserve top6 routing, swiglu_limit10 and rounding boundaries. Verify workspace cleanup under skew and repeated launches, identical capacity agreement within each EP4 group, idle-rank participation, graph buffers, and real DSpark behavior. Run target-model quality; GLM scores do not qualify this conversion.

**Cost:** High and currently unknown integration cost. A source-only eligibility check should precede any build or GPU allocation. Future cost uses P8*T_hours/N_valid_output_tokens, including memory-induced throughput loss; H200 price remains unset.

**Held out condition:** Hot-expert routing, uneven and zero-token ranks, repeated graph replay, capacity boundaries, long-context KV pressure, mixed prefill/decode, and cold/warm prefixes. Keep cache-isolated 1K/1K diagnostics separate.

**Inconclusive if:** Clamp/API/plan legality remains unknown, dependency changes are confounded, activation coverage is missing, or only different-model FP8/EP8 evidence is available. Unknown eligibility defers qualification rather than establishing ineffectiveness.

**Metrics:** Count native-FP4 fused calls over eligible expert-layer opportunities across all EP4 ranks and both replicas. Record plan IDs, per-group capacity agreement, valid/processed tokens, expert skew, staging/communication spans, workspace bytes and KV loss. Measure request TPOT=(last-first)/(output_tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact throughput, goodput/replay lag, preemption, speculation counters, and quality.

**Rejects if:** The branch cannot preserve required geometry or swiglu_limit10 without an unqualified redesign, conversion fails correctness, or precise measurements show fusion offers no useful serving improvement after workspace and KV costs.

**Supports if:** Exact native-MXFP4 semantics and EP4 correctness pass, and the fused path improves a prespecified serving metric over the best static incumbent without memory, quality, or tail guardrail failures. Confirm small gains with at least five alternating paired full-run blocks, drift checks, and run-level intervals.

**Treatment:** Source-only eligibility review first; stop pending missing clamp, geometry, or API evidence. If eligible, validate conversion and distributed kernel behavior, establish activation, screen unprofiled, diagnose the full MoE boundary separately, then perform paired confirmation and held-out evaluation. Keep workspace/plan selection fixed and validated initially.

- E384/EP4 divisibility and passing visible dimension checks do not establish clamped SwiGLU, scale representability, complete planner legality or graph safety.

**Required revision:** Deferred at 63/100, below threshold and with applicability unknown. Build a branch/API eligibility matrix for FlashInfer and the supplied SM90 DeepGEMM paths. Require clamp-active and scale-boundary reference cases.
