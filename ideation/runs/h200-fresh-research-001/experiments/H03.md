## Model statement and experiment

Port #61461's SM90 launch configuration for the delayed fused mHC post/pre-norm operation only in its supported one-to-eight-token band. The installed selector uses a GB300-derived configuration; for H5120 and confirmed hc_mult=4, the patch selects (12,10,256) instead of (2,8,128). Fewer projection tiles reduce repeated post-mapping work, offering a plausible latency saving when these launches are exposed. The author reports H20 operator improvements and a small low-concurrency serving gain, but no H200 measurement and no separated C64 gain. Count actual eligible kernel calls before porting: seq64 and DSpark k5 do not establish small-token occupancy. Retain the existing selection outside the band and evaluate this independently of coefficient overlap.

**Control:** Installed fixed synchronous mHC selection under the supplied baseline settings and native agent-f75/Mooncake workloads. If affected TF32 paths require repair, use the same verified repair in control and treatment and disclose that corrected-control delta separately.

**Correctness:** Check residuals, coefficients, and layer inputs against independent references with unchanged tolerances; summation-order changes need numerical and quality checks. Validate all warmup keys, changed-input graph replay, masked/empty cases, and unchanged larger-token fallbacks. The TF32 repair is a separate correctness prerequisite, not an attributed speedup.

**Cost:** Small source port with bounded shape tests. Exposure inspection is the cheapest falsification. Future node cost is P8*T_hours/N_valid_output_tokens; P8 and necessary runtime are unknown.

**Held out condition:** Low-load and bursty arrivals with unseen rank skew; token counts around 8/9 and 32/33; mixed and long-context traffic; separate cache-isolated 1K/1K and cold/warm prefix diagnostics.

**Inconclusive if:** Token-band telemetry is incomplete, eligible calls are too rare, compilation contaminates timing, or run variation exceeds the effect. An operator-only improvement does not establish a serving gain.

**Metrics:** Count changed-configuration hits over calls satisfying SM90, H5120, confirmed hc_mult, and 1–8 kernel tokens; also report eligible calls/all mHC calls and exposed time. Track warmup/JIT misses. Measure request TPOT=(last-first)/(output_tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact throughput, goodput/replay lag, KV/preemption, and all-rank speculation counters.

**Rejects if:** Covered eligible shapes regress, correctness fails, or sufficiently precise primary measurements exclude a useful gain. Adequately observed absence of eligible calls rejects primary-workload value, without claiming the kernel cannot help other traffic.

**Supports if:** The complete eligible operation becomes faster and a prespecified serving metric improves where the band is exposed, with no guardrail failures. Confirm small gains using at least five alternating paired full-run blocks, baseline drift checks, and run-level intervals. Report a diagnostic-only win if primary traffic lacks exposure.

**Treatment:** First verify source absence and eligible dispatch. Port only the selector/warmup change, validate correctness, screen unprofiled, diagnose operator timing separately, then confirm serving and held-out effects. No simultaneous overlap or precision change.

## Critique retained

- h200_applicability_and_correctness_required: The patch explicitly guards SM90 and returns "return 12, hidden_size // 512, 256"; H5120 satisfies that condition. The source-hunk application check succeeds. However, live hc_mult and the delayed-kernel call path are not established. Numerical, warmup, replay and fallback checks are well scoped; the application check excludes tests and native dependencies.
- Seq64 and k5 do not establish that primary traffic executes the changed band; the proposal appropriately treats this as an exposure question.
- The patch condition uses num_tokens <= 8 without a positive lower bound. Zero-token bypass must be established at the caller or explicitly tested.
- Successful source-hunk application is not a complete port, warmup-test or numerical-validation result.

**Required revision:** Deferred at 82/100 because target dispatch eligibility remains unknown. Establish the delayed caller and hc_mult, document zero-token handling, and retain the application-check scope. Keep any TF32 repair identical in both arms and report primary-workload exposure separately from low-load diagnostic value.
