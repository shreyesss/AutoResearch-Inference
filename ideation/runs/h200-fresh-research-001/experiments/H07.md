## Model statement and experiment

Port or activate DeepSelect for eligible FP32 indexer top-k calls on SM90, only after checking the installed selector and extension build. The supplied vLLM patch adds a Hopper availability path and explicit dtype, width, k and alignment checks. Its value would come from faster selection over unchanged logits, independently of attention-head padding or candidate scoring. However, the DeepSelect library's headline H20 results concern small-batch BF16 inputs, while this integration requires FP32; a commenter also reports a competing radix advantage at k=2048. Therefore, compare against the fastest eligible existing vLLM backend chosen on calibration workloads, not merely torch.topk. Preserve existing fallbacks and isolate this selector change initially with dense scoring. Any later compact-logit combination needs separate confirmation because the remaining selection workload is different.

**Control:** Supplied pinned baseline with dense scoring unchanged. Compare both deployed auto selection and the fastest eligible static native backend selected using disjoint calibration inputs. Do not use torch.topk as the sole performance comparator. Keep plan sharing and candidate-scoring changes out of the initial experiment.

**Treatment:** Begin with source, build and configuration inspection. After a compatible port or activation, establish per-rank dispatch, pass correctness, screen unprofiled native agent-f75 and fixed-schedule Mooncake at seq64, then collect separate operator diagnostics. Confirm small serving gains with at least five alternating paired full-run blocks, baseline-drift checks and intervals based on full runs.

**Metrics:** Activation: DeepSelect calls and rows divided by all eligible top-k calls and rows, with eligibility rejection reasons, dtype, k, width, alignment and row-count strata on every rank. Measure complete selection-path latency separately. Serving metrics include exact-token request TPOT, TTFT tails, throughput, historical-scorer goodput, replay lag, all-rank draft/verify/accepted deltas, KV blocks, preemption and graph fallback counts. Record missing counters and resets.

**Supports if:** The SM90 FP32 path demonstrably executes, preserves required selection semantics and model quality, beats the strongest native comparator including overhead on held-out shapes, and produces a useful paired serving improvement without unacceptable tail regression.

**Rejects if:** Equivalent eligible dispatch is already active; the necessary build or layout contracts cannot be met without erasing the benefit; correctness fails without a credible remedy; or adequately powered tests favor the best native selector or rule out useful serving improvement.

**Inconclusive if:** Only BF16 or torch.topk comparisons are available; extension architecture or selected dispatch is unverified; opportunities are insufficiently covered; or serving intervals remain wider than the predeclared useful effect.

**Held out condition:** Unseen ragged and mixed batches, short and long contexts, cold/warm prefixes, speculative row counts below and above 64, and changing per-row end bounds. Test compact candidate logits only in a later isolated combination arm; do not add estimated gains from separate experiments.

**Correctness:** Check independent reference values and tie-aware index sets, end<k padding, invalid/empty rows, supported k boundaries, input/output strides and alignment, and sorted-index semantics where consumed. Compare eager and graph replay with changing end tensors and request membership; verify allocation stability and fallback behavior. Run a model-quality subset because selection ordering or ties can affect downstream attention. Acceptance rate is diagnostic, not correctness. Map to applicable G01-G05 once defined.
