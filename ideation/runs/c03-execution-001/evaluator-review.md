4. Join each event on `(replica, counter epoch, DP step)` with exactly the expected four distinct ranks. Count the joined event once. Duplicate, missing or contradictory rank records and counter resets are errors/coverage gaps, not four independent opportunities or zero-count observations.

Preserve exact aggregate event/opportunity counters separately from the bounded inspectable sample; a sample alone cannot claim the frozen total coverage floors unless it actually includes enough complete events. 7. Verify the observation hooks are semantically inert and gated off for later service measurements. No added collectives, RNG draws, tensor mutation, device synchronization, synchronous per-token prints, or calls that change graph capture/replay. A small diagnostic-only overhead is permissible but does not enter service conclusions.
8. Run meaningful CPU/reference negative controls before use: target-capacity substitution must fail the M=5 expected bucket case; an incorrect request/probability/RNG row must fail the independent row-ownership oracle. Positive assertions must not import expected values from the modified helper or copy its implementation wholesale.

### Prospective profiling-only lower-bound method

Root archived eight prefix snapshots after profiling began at 16:36:35.884 UTC. Independent inspection of `A-profiling-prefix-snapshots/checkpoint.json` and the snapshot payloads finds their `updated_unix_s` times between 16:36:55.803 and 16:36:58.456 UTC. These payload times are assigned under the recorder lock, unlike filesystem modification time, which alone could describe a delayed write. Replica 0's asynchronous prefix observations differ by one step; this is permitted at the prefix boundary and is not permission to weaken the final all-rank join.

A new offline reader may compute, separately per replica, `final joined count − max(prefix count across the four ranks)` for online dispatches and stronger opportunities (and optionally eligible events). The largest prefix subtracts warmup plus some early profiling conservatively; the four ranks never multiply the event count. Preconditions: final full exposure passes its existing all-rank/invariant checks; all eight prefix snapshots bind the same epoch, ACTIVE marker, process/start ticks, source and frozen graph inventory as their final counterparts; histograms and counters are complete nonnegative integers with no drops/errors; every prefix event-bin count is no greater than that same rank's final bin; prefix-referenced table hashes/values remain unchanged; and final per-rank predicted totals agree. Prefix rolling digests need not agree because their boundaries differ.

Phase evidence must additionally establish that all warmup requests completed before the prefix boundary, and that the final drained archive precedes lifecycle or unrelated traffic. Thus a late warmup request cannot be mislabeled as profiling work. Payload times, phase/terminal evidence, run identity and all archive hashes must be retained. Apply the existing coverage and opportunity floors to these conservative lower bounds. Insufficient lower bounds are inconclusive for profiling-specific coverage, **not** evidence that the workload is inactive. No source/probe, endpoint, workload or service-score change is authorized by this method.

**Boundary refinement before final data interpretation:** final archive time is after profiling ends, so the initial `final − start-prefix` method can include trailing server work during drain.
