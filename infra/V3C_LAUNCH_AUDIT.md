## Approved research targets

`configs/sla-policy-v3.json`: p90 TTFT ≤1.5 s chat, ≤2.5 s agent, ≤0.75 s code, ≤12 s document; p90 TPOT ≤70 ms/token for each class; zero observed request failures; ≥30 eligible requests per class and a one-sided 95% violation-bound check. These are user-approved research targets, not measured service obligations. The 240-request count gives the 15% document class enough observations for the minimum-count rule with a margin; 36 requests cannot do that.

## What the completed run may establish

It can show concrete B0 and finalist configurations, measured latency/throughput/load curves, per-class failure and queue/KV/graph diagnostics, and provisional tradeoffs across sampled settings. It cannot by itself establish the best possible configuration, an uncertainty-qualified Pareto frontier, independently confirmed SLA capacity, real production traffic performance, prefix-cache reuse gains, MTP quality, or sealed-test generalization. Those require the separate evidence gates above.
