# C13 — Per-token FP8 Humming activation

Status: **Proposed; untested**. Updated: 2026-10-21 (Asia/Calcutta).

**Claim:** After C01 establishes a legal incumbent, compare BF16 and explicitly supported per-token FP8 with fixed GEMM/transport; quantify conversion costs and quality.

**Why:** Activation format can change expert GEMM arithmetic and memory cost, independently of backend choice and indexed/grouped execution.

**Research finding:** Fresh correctly splits this from backend selection. Humming0.1.16 block-FP8 schema mismatch and MXFP8 limitations cannot be ignored or silently bypassed.
