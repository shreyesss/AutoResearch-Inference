## Model statement and experiment

Test the existing mHC coefficient-stream overlap design on SM90 through an explicit opt-in, after repairing and validating any executed TF32 prenorm path. Installed source excludes SM90, while #60768 describes a constrained opt-in with CPU dispatch tests only. The proposed performance mechanism is to hide independent coefficient work behind other GPU work; at TP1, it cannot claim a fused TP all-reduce saving. First establish a legal overlap window and compare against the best validated synchronous fused mHC configuration. SGLang's bundled improvements do not isolate overlap, and the failed compensated-BF16 port supplies no reason to change arithmetic. Benefits, contention costs, and the suitability of the inherited sixteen-token threshold are unknown.

**Control:** Best validated synchronous fused mHC at fixed arithmetic, plus a corrected-baseline reference if the TF32 repair is required. Preserve the supplied deployment and native agent-f75/Mooncake traffic. Use identical repaired artifacts in both performance arms.

**Correctness:** Verify the register-lifetime repair in the actual generated artifact; run independent FP32/FP64 references and repeated calls across split-K, PDL and side streams. Check stream ordering, changed-input graph replay, cache/configuration hashing, and fallback behavior with GPU synchronization diagnostics. Run model quality with real DSpark. Kernel repeatability does not establish whole-model token determinism.

**Cost:** Moderate-to-high integration and correctness cost; stop before GPU work if the dependency window is absent. Future full-node cost is P8*T_hours/N_valid_output_tokens; price and duration are unknown.

**Held out condition:** Ragged rank arrivals, mixed prefill/decode, long contexts, cold/warm prefixes, empty ranks, and batches around the overlap threshold; separately exercise eager, piecewise, and large-batch fallbacks.

**Inconclusive if:** The executed TF32 artifact is unidentified, graph replay is unvalidated, eligible opportunities are sparse, or intervals remain broad. CPU dispatch tests and auxiliary-stream launches alone are insufficient.

**Metrics:** Count overlap launches over opportunities passing all non-architecture guards, and report eligible opportunities/all coefficient operations. Distinguish stream activity from useful concurrent execution and exposed waits. Across all ranks measure request TPOT=(last-first)/(output_tokens-1), TTFT tails, exact throughput, goodput/replay lag, KV/preemption, and draft/verify/accepted deltas.

**Rejects if:** No legal TP1 overlap window exists, the port has an unrepaired stream/numerical failure, or sufficiently precise measurements show overhead and contention eliminate useful improvement.

**Supports if:** Useful overlap reduces the complete critical path and improves a prespecified serving metric over the strongest synchronous comparator without quality or tail regressions. Confirm small gains with at least five alternating paired full-run blocks, drift checks, and run-level intervals.

**Treatment:** First establish dependencies, source applicability, and artifact correctness. Then enable only SM90 overlap, verify activation and numerical/graph behavior, screen unprofiled, diagnose overlap separately, and confirm serving results. Retain synchronous fallback and the initial sixteen-token threshold.
