SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#3310

Summary

Enable Engram CPU offload on H200 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash single-node Tensor Parallel, which runs at the existing TP4 default.

  • New exact-GPU h200 override under strategy_overrides.single_node_tp.hardware_overrides, adding --engram-config '{"cpu_offload":true}' and nothing else.
  • A short H200 Tensor Parallel guide section describing the cell.
  • No change to the TP map. H200 already resolves to TP4 through tp.default: 4, so adding an explicit h200: 4 would be a no-op. Leaving it out also keeps this PR free of textual conflicts with [DeepSeek] V4.1-Flash: TP2 with Engram offload on B200, B300, GB200 and GB300 #1002, which edits that map.

Why the offload

The Engram tables are 183 GiB of the checkpoint. Row-sharded in HBM on 141 GB cards they crowd out the KV pool at TP4. Moving them to pinned host DRAM, read through UVA with unchanged output, frees 23.6 GiB per rank, 94 GiB across the replica.

Measured on the InferenceX AgentX sweep, from the c1 server log of run 35494445931:

Quantity Value
Engram offloaded per rank 23.6 GiB
Resident weights per GPU 81.2 GiB
KV per GPU 38.5 GiB
KV tokens (replica) 20.0M

No scheduler caps are needed at this replica size. Unlike the 180 GB B200 TP2 cell, the indexer's logits buffer fits alongside the weights with room to spare, so --max-num-batched-tokens and --max-cudagraph-capture-size stay at their upstream defaults.

Validation

  • InferenceX AgentX sweep green at 17 of 17 jobs, covering concurrency 1 through 128 plus evals, and the existing TP8 points re-ran green under the same config key: SemiAnalysisAI/InferenceX#3310, run 35494445931.
  • node scripts/build-recipes-api.mjs renders the H200 page at --tensor-parallel-size 4 with the Engram flag; H100 (TP8) and B300 (TP2) are unchanged.

Relationship to #1002

#1002 adds TP2 with Engram offload for the Blackwell SKUs. This PR is independent: it touches only Hopper, adds its override after the b300 block rather than before it, and makes no TP-map edit, so the two can merge in either order.
