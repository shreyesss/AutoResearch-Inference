# X02 — Evaluate a Hopper-native MXFP4 expert kernel route

Lane: **exploratory**. Live-layout priority: **not ranked**. Status: conditional plan; no GPU test or measured gain.

## Problem and causal hypothesis

A native Hopper expert kernel could avoid Marlin/Humming overhead, but existing proposals use different expert layouts and precisions.

## Applicability to our H200 deployment

DeepGEMM473 evidence uses H20 EP8 and4096/2048; #62603 requires FlashInfer0.7.2 versus installed0.7.0.post1 and block-FP8 weights. Reject unsupported shape/layout before full-model runs.

## Isolated control and treatment

First integrate and validate one MXFP4 route for hidden5120/intermediate2304/EP4. #62603 block-FP8 and #53709 weight conversion are separate alternatives, never interchangeable prerequisites.

## Evaluation and predicted mechanism

E1: native agent-f75; E2: frozen Mooncake median/burst/cache-heavy. H200 capacity discovery and held-out confirmation in PROTOCOL.md.

Measure: Expert kernel parity/time, packing and communication, weight/HBM expansion, KV capacity, quality and SLO goodput.

## Why an additional evaluation is or is not needed

Reuse E1/E2 only after kernel/reference and task-quality gates. Weight-conversion route requires capacity and precision evaluation that performance traces cannot provide.

## Decision and falsification

Apply [the preregistered protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Confirm path and missing behavior before spending GPU time. Support requires correctness, predicted mechanism movement and a useful held-out SLO outcome. Reject an active, well-measured change with no useful endpoint gain or a correctness/tail regression. Mark absent-path, underpowered or failed-replay cases inapplicable/inconclusive; do not label them disproven. Correctness lanes establish only scoped correctness.

## Evidence limits

Not cherry-pick-ready. FP8 weights can nearly double expert-weight storage relative to MXFP4 and shrink KV headroom.
